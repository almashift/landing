About AlmaShift

AlmaShift is an online academy for hands-on tech skills, based in Trivandrum, India. Expert trainers teach DevOps and cloud-native technologies through short video lessons and labs on real tools.

Courses are self-paced or run as live online batches at learn.almashift.com. Every completed course earns a digital certificate. All classes are online.
