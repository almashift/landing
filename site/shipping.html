Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short: everything AlmaShift sells is delivered online. Nothing is shipped, and there are no delivery charges.

What you receive

AlmaShift courses are digital: video lessons, hands-on labs, quizzes, assignments and downloadable materials, delivered through our learning platform at learn.almashift.com. Live batches are delivered as scheduled online sessions. We don't sell or ship any physical products.

When you get access

Self-paced courses

Access starts immediately after your payment is confirmed, usually within a few minutes. The course appears under My Courses when you log in, and we email you a confirmation.

Live batches

Your seat is confirmed immediately after payment. The joining link, schedule and any preparation material are available on the batch page and sent by email before the first session. Sessions take place on the dates and times shown on the batch page (in IST unless stated otherwise).

Certificates

Certificates of completion are issued digitally on the platform once you meet the course's completion requirements, and can be downloaded or shared online. Printed certificates are not sent by post.

Delivery charges and locations

There are no shipping or delivery charges. Courses can be accessed from anywhere with an internet connection, on a computer, tablet or phone. Some labs work best on a computer with an up-to-date browser.

If you don't receive access

If your course or batch doesn't appear in your account within 2 hours of a successful payment, email support@almashift.com with your payment ID or receipt. We'll fix it within 1 business day, or refund you in full under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Questions

Email support@almashift.com, or see our Contact page.
